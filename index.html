Drag across the waveform to select bars; click to play from a bar. Click a block on the strip to go there. A cue's ⟲ button switches it between loop, ⤨ riff (its bars in a varied order, like a band vamping; 🎲 for another riff) and → once. Keys: Space play/stop, ← → back/next cue, 1-9 jump to a cue, L loop the selection, R play on, C add a cue.
